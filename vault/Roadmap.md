## 1. Story Mode Colosseums 5-8 (levels 35-50)

Colosseums 1-4 (levels 1-34) are done. See [Story Mode](StoryMode.md).

- New enemies and maps.
- A boss at the end of every Colosseum. Colosseums can have any number of levels (`COLOSSEUM_SIZES`). 16 levels are left before The Sun, so the sizes of Colosseums 5-8 still need deciding.
- The last level is **Level 50: The Sun**.
- New bosses get classical or medieval, Souls-like music, not cyber or synth.
