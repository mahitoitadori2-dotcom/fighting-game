The Enlightened One sends the hero to **Orbis Terrarum**; to get a ticket you must travel through every land. (Details of the Chase lore: [Story Mode](StoryMode.md), "Enlightened Chase lore".)

## Orbis Terrarum

- A part of **the Storm**, and its **2nd entrance** - the only **secret** one.
- An **imaginary island** with **one hut**. **No one has ever gone inside the hut.**
- Home of a race called **the Learners**, in three ranks:
  - **Students** - anyone who wants to learn from a Trainer. A Student can be ANYONE (not only a Learner).
  - **Trainers** - fully **blue**. Only Learners can be Trainers.
  - **Masters** - fully **yellow**. A Trainer becomes a Master once they have taught someone everything.
