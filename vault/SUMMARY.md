# Universal Beatdown - Summary

Universal Beatdown is a free 2D platform fighter, made in Godot 4.7.

## State

| | |
|---|---|
| **Version** | v0.2.0 (latest GitHub Release) |
| **Play online** | https://mahitoitadori2-dotcom.github.io/fighting-game/ |
| **Downloads** | Windows, macOS, Linux and Web zips on the [Releases page](https://github.com/mahitoitadori2-dotcom/fighting-game/releases) |
| **Builds** | Automatic. Every push builds all platforms. A push to `main` updates the website. A `v*` tag makes a release. |
| **Playable** | Versus (6 fighters incl. the **Anti Architect** - Versus only, never in Story; 7 test enemies, 4 Fights-mode bosses incl. Kokusen, and 33 Story enemies and bosses that unlock when you meet them in Story) and Story Mode, all 5 Colosseums (levels 1-50: 6 / 9 / 11 / 8 / 16 levels). Colosseum 4 is played as the Architect, level 32 The Shining is optional, Colosseum 5 has a hero choice (switchable on the board) + a HARD CPU partner with ultimates and a flask, and ends with The Sun. Plus 5 **Boss Extensions** (BC, AC, FM, AG, VM: 15 levels, 16 new enemies, 5 new bosses, 10 new arenas). After Colosseum 5 comes a new story part, **THE ENLIGHTENED CHASE** (levels 51-62: solo chase, Counselors + Camel Riders, mini-boss The Enlightened First Prime, boss The Enlightened One, ends with the dark cloud), then **DEMILAND** (level 63: an OPEN-WORLD level, 5-15 min, Souls rules, CamTon checkpoints, day / night, a map, 3 bosses whose artifacts open the Ancient Door). The **Anti Architect** can play every Story level and extension solo (board toggle). **Achievements** (139, with artwork, pop-ups, a grid menu, Story challenge badges, Colosseum 5 SOLO mode and Galaxy skins as the ultimate reward). **Story dialogue** in a typed text box with portraits and voice blips (Colosseum 1-5 openings and bosses, every Enlightened Chase level, lines from the real lore) |
| **Audio** | Music: real orchestral instruments (Souls / Hollow Knight style, since 2026-11-12). Sound effects: 24 of 36 are real Kenney CC0 recordings (since 2026-11-13), 12 are still placeholders |
| **Tested** | By CPU-controlled matches and in Chrome. Not tested by a human player or on a real iPhone. |

## Next step

Play Colosseum 5, the Enlightened Chase and DemiLand yourself and tune the bosses. Listen to the new music, sound effects and voice blips. Test the web game on an iPhone for 5+ minutes (no crash, sound works, silent in the background). Tag a new release: v0.2.0 does not have the work after 2026-11-08.
