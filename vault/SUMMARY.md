# Universal Beatdown - Summary

Universal Beatdown is a free 2D platform fighter, made in Godot 4.7.

## State

| | |
|---|---|
| **Version** | v0.2.0 (latest GitHub Release) |
| **Play online** | https://mahitoitadori2-dotcom.github.io/fighting-game/ |
| **Downloads** | Windows, macOS, Linux and Web zips on the [Releases page](https://github.com/mahitoitadori2-dotcom/fighting-game/releases) |
| **Builds** | Automatic. Every push builds all platforms. A push to `main` updates the website. A `v*` tag makes a release. |
| **Playable** | Versus (6 fighters incl. the new **Anti Architect**, 7 test enemies, 4 Fights-mode bosses incl. Kokusen) and Story Mode, all 5 Colosseums (levels 1-50: 6 / 9 / 11 / 8 / 16 levels). Colosseum 4 is played as the Architect, level 32 The Shining is optional, Colosseum 5 has a hero choice (switchable on the board) + a HARD CPU partner with ultimates and a flask, and ends with The Sun. Plus 5 **Boss Extensions** (BC, AC, FM, AG, VM: 15 levels, 16 new enemies, 5 new bosses, 10 new arenas). After Colosseum 5 comes a new story part, **THE ENLIGHTENED CHASE** (levels 51-62: solo chase, Counselors + Camel Riders, mini-boss The Enlightened First Prime, boss The Enlightened One, ends with the dark cloud), then **DEMILAND** (level 63: an OPEN-WORLD level, 5-15 min, Souls rules, CamTon checkpoints, day / night, a map, 3 bosses whose artifacts open the Ancient Door). The **Anti Architect** can play every Story level and extension solo (board toggle). **Achievements** (139, with artwork, pop-ups, a grid menu, Story challenge badges, Colosseum 5 SOLO mode and Galaxy skins as the ultimate reward) |
| **Audio** | Music: real orchestral instruments (Souls / Hollow Knight style, since 2026-11-12). Sound effects: placeholders |
| **Tested** | By CPU-controlled matches and in Chrome. Not tested by a human player or on a real iPhone. |
