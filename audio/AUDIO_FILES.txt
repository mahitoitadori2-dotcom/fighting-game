UNIVERSAL BEATDOWN - AUDIO FILES
================================
Drop your sounds here with EXACTLY these names. Any of .ogg / .wav / .mp3 works
(SoundManager tries .ogg first, then .wav, then .mp3). Placeholder .wav files are
already here so nothing is silent; replace them whenever you like.
After adding files, open the Godot editor once so it imports them, then re-export Web.

Tips: SFX = short .wav (or .ogg). MUSIC = .ogg (much smaller than .wav, loops cleanly).
A file you delete is simply silent - the game never crashes on a missing sound.
